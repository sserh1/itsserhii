Short notes on books I've finished, newest at the top.

## Designing Data-Intensive Applications

Martin Kleppmann. The book that finally made replication, partitioning and transactions feel like one connected topic instead of a list of buzzwords. The chapter on the trouble with distributed systems is worth rereading every year.

## The Pragmatic Programmer

David Thomas, Andrew Hunt. Less a book about code than about habits. A lot of it sounds obvious now, which probably means it worked.

## A Philosophy of Software Design

John Ousterhout. Deep modules, shallow interfaces. I disagree with some of it, and that's exactly why it's useful: it made me put words to why.
