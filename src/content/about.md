Over the course of my career, I've been building web and mobile apps, distributed systems, runtimes, compilers, virtual machines, and now working close to blockchain internals and zkRollups. ## Why this blog?
The internet is getting a weird place to be. It's getting flooded with AI-generated content from all corners, and this blog is basically a bunch of ideas and personal experiences which are, you know, mine.

I love explaining things I find interesting to people who aren't necessarily familiar with the field I'm working in. And it's a great exercise — it challenges your own understanding. There are so many things I wish I could get my hands on, dig deeper into, and find the right explanations for. But overall, even if there's just one person whom these posts help understand something better, I'll consider my work done.

But this blog is not only about programming. I consider sharing valuable personal life experiences just as important as sharing professional ones. So, I'll also write about traveling, hobbies, literature, and more. And yeah, for this particular project, I use AI only to correct my English grammar and validate the accuracy of some claims, and nothing else.
